Privacy Policy

Hermes Personal Assistant — single-user, locally operated desktop software

This Privacy Policy explains what information Hermes Personal Assistant ("the application", "Hermes") collects, why it collects it, how it is used and stored, how long it is kept, and how it can be deleted. It applies to the one person who installs and operates the application on their own computer ("the owner", "you"). The application has no other users.

1. Summary

  • Hermes accesses your Google data only to perform tasks you ask for, on your own machine
  • There is no server operated by the author; no data is sent to the author
  • Nothing is sold, rented, shared, or used for advertising or profiling
  • Google user data is never used to train or improve generalized AI models
  • You can revoke access and delete local data at any time (section 9)

2. Information the application accesses

With your permission, granted through Google's OAuth consent screen, Hermes accesses the following data from your own Google Account:

  • Gmail (gmail.readonly, gmail.modify, gmail.send, gmail.settings.basic) — message headers, bodies and attachments, so Hermes can summarize your inbox, find messages you ask about, label or archive them on request, and send mail you have explicitly approved. The settings scope is used only to create and manage your own mail filters when you ask.
  • Google Calendar (calendar, calendar.events) — your calendars and events, so Hermes can build your daily schedule, detect conflicts, and create, change or delete events you request.
  • Google Tasks (tasks, tasks.readonly) — your task lists, so Hermes can read and update your tasks.
  • Google Drive and Docs (drive.readonly, documents.readonly) — read-only access to files and documents you name or search for. Hermes cannot modify or delete anything in Drive.
  • Basic account identity (userinfo.email, userinfo.profile) — your email address and display name, used to address summaries and confirm which account is connected.

Hermes does not collect your contacts, location, browsing history, health data, financial account data, or passwords. It never asks for or stores your Google password.

3. How the information is used

Data is used only to carry out the functions you request in conversation with the application:

  • Producing morning and evening briefings from your mail, calendar and tasks
  • Answering questions about your own messages, schedule and documents
  • Drafting and sending email that you review and approve before it is sent
  • Creating or updating events and tasks you ask for
  • Sending notifications and reminders to you through your chosen messaging channel

Data is never used for advertising, profiling, scoring, resale, or any purpose unrelated to these functions.

4. Where processing happens

Hermes runs on your own computer. Your Google data is processed there. The author of this application operates no server, no database and no analytics service, and receives none of your data.

To summarize or draft text, the application sends the relevant excerpt to a large language model provider that you select and configure, using your account and that provider's terms. That transmission goes directly from your machine to the provider. You control which provider is used, can change it at any time, and can run the application with a locally hosted model so that no data leaves your machine.

5. Storage and security

  • An OAuth credential (access token and refresh token) is stored in a file on your computer, readable only by your operating-system user account (owner-only permissions)
  • Copies of message, calendar and task content may be written to local logs and local databases created by the application, on your own disk
  • Access to those files is controlled by your operating system's user permissions and disk encryption, if enabled
  • No Google user data is stored by any third party on behalf of this application

6. Retention

  • OAuth credential — kept until you revoke access or delete the file
  • Local logs and caches — retained on a rolling basis, typically days to weeks, and deletable by you at any time
  • Conversation history — stored locally so the application has context; kept until you delete it
  • Nothing is retained by the author, because the author never receives it

7. Sharing and disclosure

Hermes does not sell, rent, trade, or transfer Google user data to any third party. It does not share data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers. Data is disclosed only:

  • To the language-model provider you configured, for the purpose of generating the response you asked for
  • If you explicitly instruct the application to send information somewhere (for example, an email you approve)
  • If required by applicable law

No human other than you reads your Google user data.

8. Google API Services — Limited Use

Hermes' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Google user data is used only to provide or improve the user-facing features described above
  • Google user data is not transferred to third parties except as needed to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice
  • Google user data is not used for advertising
  • Google user data is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models
  • No human reads Google user data except the owner, for support he requests, or where required by law

9. Your choices: revoking access and deleting data

  • Revoke access: myaccount.google.com/permissions → select the application → Remove access. This immediately invalidates the stored credential and the application stops working until you authorize it again.
  • Delete local data: delete the credential file and the application's data directory on your computer. Instructions are in the application's documentation.
  • Request deletion or ask a question: email scott.jensen2@gmail.com. Requests are acknowledged and completed within 30 days.

10. Children

The application is not directed to children under 13 and is not used by anyone other than its adult owner.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Continued use of the application after a change constitutes acceptance.
